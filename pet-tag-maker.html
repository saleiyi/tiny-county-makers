Pet ID tag shape and size guide

The maker sizes the tag by its longest side and fits the photo inside whichever silhouette you choose. The collar hole is punched at the top centre, and the pet name and phone number climb from the base, so upload the photo first and read the size line under the preview before you download.

Questions

Pet ID tag FAQ

How do I make a pet ID tag online?

Upload a pet photo, pick a circle, oval, rounded or classic tag, and choose a 3, 4 or 5 cm long side. The maker frames the photo inside the silhouette, punches the collar hole and stacks the pet name and your phone number on the face, then you download a transparent 300 DPI PNG or an SVG cut path for free.

Is the pet tag maker free?

Yes. Uploading a photo, previewing the shape and downloading the PNG or SVG are free, with no account and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser. A hand-finished acrylic pet tag is an optional paid service.

What should I put on a dog tag or a cat tag?

The pet name, then one contact line such as a mobile number. On a 3 cm tag keep the name to one short word so it stays readable, and use a phone number rather than a full address: anyone who finds the pet can call without reading a paragraph.

What size pet tag should I order?

The maker sizes the tag by its longest side. 3 cm suits a cat or a small puppy, 4 cm is the everyday size for a medium dog, and 5 cm gives a large dog, or a tag carrying a long phone number, more room for the photo and the text.

Can I order the physical acrylic pet tag?

Yes. One tag is $14.99, two are $24.99 and three are $32.99 before shipping. Send the pet photo through the quote form, approve the proof, and we send a PayPal invoice only after you are happy with the design.
