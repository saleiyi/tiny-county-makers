Reference

Photo keychain size and shape guide

The finished size is the longest side of the acrylic piece. Choose the shape first, then the size, because a circle crops more of a rectangular photo than a rounded rectangle does.

Size or shapeWorks well forWatch out for
4 cmSmall keepsakes, spare keys, simple logosFine detail and small text disappear
5 cmA single face, pet or couple photoVery wide photos leave empty acrylic on the sides
6 cmTwo people, a group or a detailed sceneHeavier on a keyring; keep the top hole balanced
Rounded rectangleMost photos, names, dates and short messagesUse a slightly thicker border on irregular edges
CircleOne centered face, pet, icon or logoLandscape photos lose their left and right edges
SquareInstagram-style photos, patterns and bold artLeave breathing room around the subject

What makes a good photo keychain image

  • Use the highest-resolution original you have. A phone photo is fine when the face or subject is sharp.
  • Keep one clear subject near the center. A busy background competes with the subject at 4 to 6 cm.
  • Check the crop in the preview before you download. The tool shows exactly what fits inside the acrylic shape.
  • Choose a shape that matches the photo: rounded rectangle for groups and text, circle for a centered face, square for a clean modern look.

Questions

Photo keychain FAQ

How do I make a photo keychain online?

Upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo, choose the finished size and shape, then download a transparent 300 DPI PNG. If you want the physical acrylic keychain, send the design or photo through the quote form and a person will reply with a proof and price.

Is this photo keychain maker free?

Yes. The maker, preview and PNG download are free, need no account and add no watermark. Your photo is processed inside your browser. A physical keychain is an optional paid service.

What photo works best for an acrylic photo keychain?

Use a sharp, well-lit photo with one clear subject near the center. Square or portrait photos work especially well because the subject stays easy to see at 4 to 6 cm.

Which keychain shape should I choose?

Rounded rectangle is the standard acrylic look and keeps landscape photos readable. Circle works best for a centered face, pet or logo, and square gives a clean, modern frame for Instagram-style photos.

What does a physical photo keychain cost?

One keychain is $14.99, two are $24.99 and three are $32.99 before shipping. We confirm the artwork, exact size, destination and shipping cost, then send a PayPal invoice only after you approve the proof.
