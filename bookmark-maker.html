Bookmark shape and size guide

The maker sizes the bookmark by its longest side and fits the photo inside whichever silhouette you choose. The tassel hole is punched near the top centre, and the caption sits at the base, so upload the photo first and read the size line under the preview before you download.

Questions

Acrylic bookmark FAQ

How do I make a bookmark online?

Upload a photo, pick a classic, arch, ribbon-notch or pointed acrylic bookmark, and choose a 15, 18 or 20 cm long side. The maker frames the photo inside the slab, punches the tassel hole and lays the caption on the face, then you download a transparent 300 DPI PNG or an SVG cut path for free.

Is the bookmark maker free?

Yes. Uploading a photo, previewing the shape and downloading the PNG or SVG are free, with no account and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser. A hand-finished acrylic bookmark is an optional paid service.

What should I put on an acrylic bookmark?

A short title such as a name, a book club or a single word, then an optional second line for a date or a note. Keep the title under about 28 characters and the second line under about 32 so the maker can set both lines large enough to read against the photo.

What size should an acrylic bookmark be?

The maker sizes the bookmark by its longest side. 15 cm is a compact paperback marker, 18 cm is the everyday hardcover size, and 20 cm gives a large hardback or a journal more room for the photo and the caption.

Can I order the physical acrylic bookmark?

Yes. One bookmark is $14.99, two are $24.99 and three are $32.99 before shipping. Send the photo through the quote form, approve the proof, and we send a PayPal invoice only after you are happy with the design.
