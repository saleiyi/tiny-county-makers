What makes a good acrylic photo ornament

  • Start with a bright, sharp photo. The silhouette crops to the shape, so keep the face or pet near the middle.
  • Match the shape to the subject. A heart or an arch suits a portrait; a star or a hexagon suits a bold graphic photo.
  • Keep an engraved name short. Up to 24 characters fit, but a first name or a single date reads best at ornament size.
  • Choose the size after you see the preview. The longest side is fixed at 6, 8 or 10 cm and the other side follows the shape.
  • Use the SVG cut path if you plan to laser cut at home. It contains the finished silhouette and the hanging hole, so it is ready to send to a cutter.

What is the best photo for an acrylic ornament?

Use a bright, sharp photo with one clear subject near the middle, because the silhouette crops to the shape. Faces, pets and head-and-shoulder portraits work best, and a simple background keeps an engraved name readable.
