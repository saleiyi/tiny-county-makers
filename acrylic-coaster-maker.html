Three steps

How to make a photo coaster

Pick a photo that reads at a glance

A coaster is seen from above while you sit at the table, so one clear subject beats a wide scene. A square photo fills either blank with the least cropping, and a bright photo prints best on a white acrylic base.

Choose the blank and the size

Square blanks show more of a scene and stack neatly; round blanks follow a mug. Set 9, 10 or 11 cm on the longest side and watch how much of the photo survives the crop.

Download or order a set

Take the free 300 DPI PNG and the SVG cut path anywhere you like, or send the photo through the quote form if you want a set made — useful for new homes, weddings, teams or cafés.

Reference

Acrylic coaster size guide

Coaster sizes are given as the longest finished side. A round and a square blank of the same size look different on a table: the round one reads smaller because its corners are gone.

Long sideWorks well forWatch out for
9 cmEspresso cups, candles, small gift setsOne face only; skip long captions
10 cmThe everyday mug, one portrait, a petVery wide photos lose their edges to the crop
11 cmGroup shots, recipes, logos, sets of fourNeeds more table room; heavier in a stack

Getting a good print

  • Keep faces away from the rim; the acrylic border takes a little from every edge.
  • Acrylic prints on a white base, so very dark photos look muddy. Brighten slightly before sending.
  • For a set, keep the same size and blank so the coasters look like a family.
  • Want the cut to follow the subject instead of the blank? Generate a cutline from a cut-out PNG first.

Acrylic coaster FAQ

How do I make a photo coaster?

Upload the photo, choose a square or round blank, set the 9, 10 or 11 cm finished size and check the crop in the live preview. Then download a transparent 300 DPI PNG or an SVG cut path for free.

What size should an acrylic coaster be?

A 9 cm coaster suits espresso cups, small candles and gift sets. 10 cm is the everyday size that fits a mug with room to spare. 11 cm gives a photo more room and reads better across a table.

Square or round coaster, which should I choose?

A round blank follows a mug and a round photo. A square blank shows more of a scene and stacks neatly, so it suits logos, recipes and sets of four. Both keep the same footprint on the coaster.
