Questions before you create

Custom acrylic keychain FAQ

How much does a custom acrylic keychain cost?

One keychain is estimated at $14.99, two at $24.99, and three at $32.99 with standard tracked shipping included. Remote destinations, unusual sizes, taxes, and duties are confirmed before invoicing.

What size can I choose?

The standard finished long-side options are 4 cm, 5 cm, and 6 cm. The dimension describes the acrylic body and excludes the metal ring and chain.

Can I make a keychain from a photo?

Yes. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WEBP, keep it rectangular or manually erase the background, then preview and download the design before requesting production.

Do I have to order?

No. The design tool, the preview, and the downloads are free. No signup, no email address, and no purchase are required.
