Cake topper style and size guide

The maker sizes the topper by its finished width and lets the height follow the wording, so a short name cuts a tall piece and a long message cuts a wide one. Read the size line under the preview before you download.

Questions

Cake topper FAQ

How do I make a cake topper online?

Type the wording, choose a lettering style and pick a welded bar, bare letters or a round plaque, then set the finished width to 10, 12 or 15 cm. The maker traces the letters into a cut path, and you download a transparent 300 DPI PNG plus an SVG for free.

Is the cake topper maker free?

Yes. Typing the wording, previewing the topper and downloading the PNG or the SVG cut path are free, with no account and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser. A hand-finished acrylic cake topper is an optional paid service.

Why does a cake topper need a welding bar?

Two words such as Happy Birthday only stay connected if the letters share one piece of material. The bar style draws a horizontal band under the wording that overlaps the lowest letters, so the whole topper cuts and ships as one piece of acrylic instead of a pile of loose letters.

What size should an acrylic cake topper be?

The maker sizes the topper by its finished width. 10 cm suits a small 6 inch cake, 12 cm is the everyday 8 inch cake size, and 15 cm fills the top of a 10 inch or tiered cake. The height follows the wording, so a long message makes a taller piece.

Can I order the physical acrylic cake topper?

Yes. One topper is $14.99, two are $24.99 and three are $32.99 before shipping. Send the wording and the style through the quote form, approve the proof, and we send a PayPal invoice only after you are happy with the design.
