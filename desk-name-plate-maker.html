Reference

Desk name plate sizes and finishes

An office desk name plate is measured in inches rather than centimetres. The plate is 2 inches tall in every size, so only the width changes, and the width is what decides how much copy fits next to the edge.

Plate sizeWorks well forWatch out for
2 x 8 in (20 x 5 cm)A short name, a first name only, a reception counterA long company name has to shrink
2 x 10 in (25 x 5 cm)The everyday office desk: name plus titleNo room for a wide logo and a long line together
2 x 12 in (30 x 5 cm)A name, a title, a company line and a logoNeeds a deeper desk or shelf to sit level

Which acrylic finish should I choose?

  • Black acrylic carries white etched lettering and reads from across a room, which suits a front desk or a door sign.
  • Clear acrylic is the classic engraved office plate: light passes through and the lettering sits on a barely visible face.
  • Frosted acrylic softens glare on a bright desk while keeping the lettering crisp.

What the download contains

  • The name, title and company line, set as etched type on the plate face at the crop you approved in the preview.
  • A transparent 300 DPI PNG, plus an SVG cut path named Cutline for the rounded plate outline.
  • An optional logo, fitted whole on the right of the plate, with no cropping and no background.
  • No watermark, so the file can go straight into a laser or a print job.

Getting a good engraving

  • Keep the name to two or three words. A shorter name is set larger and reads from further away.
  • Upload a logo as a PNG with a transparent or plain background, not as a photo of a sign.
  • Black and frosted acrylic engrave lighter than the face; clear acrylic needs the letters to be deep enough to catch the light.
  • Want a photo instead of type? Make a photo keychain or an acrylic photo block from the same engine.

Questions

Desk name plate FAQ

How do I make a desk name plate?

Type the name, the title and the company line, choose a 2x8, 2x10 or 2x12 inch acrylic plate and add a logo if you have one. The preview updates as you type, and the transparent 300 DPI PNG plus an SVG cut path are free to download.

What size is a desk name plate?

A desk name plate is 2 inches tall. The common widths are 8, 10 and 12 inches, which is about 20, 25 and 30 cm. Pick 8 inches for a short name, 10 inches for a full name with a title, and 12 inches when a logo or a long company name shares the plate.

Can I add a logo to a desk name plate?

Yes. Upload a PNG or JPG and the tool places the mark on the right of the plate, scaled to fit whole so nothing is cropped. Leave the upload empty when the name should sit on its own.

Is the desk name plate maker free?

Yes. Typing the plate, previewing it and downloading the PNG or SVG are free, with no account and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser. A laser engraved acrylic plate is an optional paid service.

Can I order the physical desk name plate?

Yes. Send the name, the title you want engraved and the size through the quote form, and we will reply with sizes, options and a price before anything is paid.
