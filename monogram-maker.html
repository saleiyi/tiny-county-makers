What the styles print

  • Classic three initials sets the middle letter larger. Type whole names and the surname is moved into that middle spot the traditional way, while initials typed in the order you want are printed as they were written. More than three names are trimmed to the first three.
  • Two initials keeps the ampersand between the letters, which is the wedding and anniversary arrangement most people are after.
  • Single initial centres one letter, which is the version that stays legible at small sizes on a tag, a keyring or a glass.
  • Block four initials sets one letter from each of up to four names in an even, firm row, which keeps a whole name in order instead of rearranging it.
  • Wedding script keeps letters, digits, an ampersand and a full stop, so a couple’s two names print in a joined hand.
  • Number date keeps only the digits and groups up to eight of them, which turns 09 16 2026 into a clean wedding or anniversary date.

Which initial goes in the middle of a monogram?

Which monogram style should I choose?

Classic three initials suits a personal monogram and lifts the surname in the middle. Two initials with an ampersand is the one to reach for on a wedding or anniversary piece. What is the difference between the circle, oval, diamond, shield and square frames?
