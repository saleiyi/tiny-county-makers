Luggage tag shape and size guide

The maker sizes the tag by its longest side and fits the photo inside whichever silhouette you choose. The strap hole is punched at the top centre, and the name and contact line climb from the base, so upload the photo first and read the size line under the preview before you download.

Questions

Luggage tag FAQ

How do I make a luggage tag online?

Upload a photo, pick a rounded, classic, circle or oval acrylic tag, and choose a 7, 9 or 11 cm long side. The maker frames the photo inside the shape, punches the strap hole and stacks your name and contact line on the face, then you download a transparent 300 DPI PNG or an SVG cut path for free.

Is the luggage tag maker free?

Yes. Uploading a photo, previewing the shape and downloading the PNG or SVG are free, with no account and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser. A hand-finished acrylic tag is an optional paid service.

What should I put on a luggage tag?

A surname or a first initial, then one contact line such as a mobile number or an email address. Keep the name short so the maker can set it large, and keep the contact line under about 30 characters so it stays readable on a 7 cm tag.

What size should an acrylic luggage tag be?

The maker sizes the tag by its longest side. 7 cm suits a cabin bag or a child backpack, 9 cm is the everyday checked-bag tag, and 11 cm gives a duffel or an instrument case more room for a photo and two lines of text.

Can I order the physical acrylic luggage tag?

Yes. One tag is $14.99, two are $24.99 and three are $32.99 before shipping. Send the photo through the quote form, approve the proof, and we send a PayPal invoice only after you are happy with the design.
