How to turn a photo into a coloring page

Upload a photo or start from the blank sheet

Add a PNG, JPG or WebP up to 12 MB, or press the sample button to try the tool. The picture is fitted inside the printer margin, so a shot with the subject in the middle converts best. You can also download the empty sheet first and draw on it.

Choose the paper, the style and the line weight

Pick US Letter or A4 and portrait or landscape, then choose clean outlines or a pencil sketch, set the detail from simple to detailed, and set the printed line weight from fine to bold. The preview redraws every time you change a setting.

Download the 300 DPI page and print it

Press Download PNG and the whole sheet is drawn again at 300 DPI, so the printed line weight is exactly what you picked. Print at 100 percent with no scaling. No watermark, no signup, and nothing you upload leaves your device.

A coloring page is only useful if the shapes are closed, the lines are dark enough to follow and the paper is the right size. The maker therefore works at the real print resolution: it converts the photo to greyscale, smooths it so small noise does not turn into scribble, then either divides the picture into a few tones and inks the seam between them or keeps just the tonal edges, and finally thickens every line to the weight you chose in millimetres.

Choosing a line weight

  • Fine (0.5 mm) suits a detailed drawing, adult coloring books and anything that will be painted rather than filled with markers.
  • Medium (0.9 mm) is the safe default: dark enough to read on a photocopy, light enough not to close up small shapes.
  • Bold (1.4 mm) is the easiest weight for a young child or for a page that will be enlarged again after printing.

Choosing a detail level

  • Simple smooths hardest and keeps the fewest tones, which suits a portrait head-and-shoulders shot or a child's coloring page.
  • Balanced keeps enough edges to recognise a face or a pet while the shapes stay large enough to color in.
  • Detailed keeps the most tonal edges, so more of the original photo survives - best for buildings, plants and adult coloring pages.

Photos that convert well

  • Pets work best when the animal fills the frame and the background is plain, because fur turns into readable strokes.
  • People convert cleanly from a head-and-shoulders portrait with even light; a hard side light turns into a heavy shadow line.
  • Houses and landmarks hold their roof lines, windows and railings, which makes them the most recognizable conversions of all.
  • Children's drawings, logos and pets from above also work, because a flat subject with clear edges posterizes into tidy shapes.

Printing and privacy

  • Print at 100 percent with no page scaling or fit-to-page, or the margin and the line weight will both drift.
  • Plain 80 gsm paper is fine for a quick test; 120-160 gsm paper takes felt tips and water-based markers without ghosting through.
  • The tool is a static page and the conversion happens in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded to a server and never stored.

Photo to coloring page FAQ

How do I turn a photo into a coloring page?

Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP up to 12 MB, pick US Letter or A4, choose clean outlines or a pencil sketch, then set the detail and the line weight and download the 300 DPI PNG. The photo is read in your browser, the line art is drawn on your device, and the finished page is saved straight to your downloads folder.

Is the photo to coloring page converter free?

Yes. Uploading a photo, changing the paper, the orientation, the style, the detail, the line weight and the invert option, and downloading the finished page as a 300 DPI PNG are all free. There is no account, no watermark and no limit, and the picture never leaves your device.

Do I have to upload my photo to a server?

No. The tool is a static page and every step happens inside your browser: the photo is read into memory, converted to greyscale, smoothed, posterized and traced, and the finished page is written straight to a download. Nothing is sent anywhere, which is why it is safe to use with family photos and pictures of children.

What is the difference between clean outlines and pencil sketch?

Clean outlines posterizes the photo into a few tones and inks the seam between them, which closes the shapes the way a printed coloring book does. Pencil sketch runs a difference of Gaussians over the picture, which keeps only the tonal edges and leaves the rest of the paper open, so it reads as a hand-drawn study rather than as a page to fill in.

What size is the printable page?

US Letter comes out at 2550 x 3300 pixels in portrait and A4 at 2480 x 3508 pixels, both at 300 DPI, and switching to landscape swaps the two numbers. The line art keeps a 1.27 cm printer margin on every side, so a home printer can print the page at 100 percent without clipping the edges.

How should I print the coloring page?

Open the PNG and print it at 100 percent with no page scaling, on plain paper for a quick test or on 120-160 gsm paper and markers for a finished page. The line art is near-black on white, so it prints quickly and cheaply. For a whole booklet or a batch of sheets, send the photos and we will quote the printed run.
