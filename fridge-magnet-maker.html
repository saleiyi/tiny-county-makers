Photo magnet size guide

Magnet sizes are given as the longest finished side. Two designs with the same long side can look very different — a square magnet feels much larger than a wide strip.

Getting a good print

  • Keep faces away from the rounded corners; that is where heads get clipped.
  • Magnet material is printed on a white base, so very dark photos print muddy. Brighten slightly before sending.
  • For a set, reuse the same size and corner radius so the magnets look like a family.
  • Want it shaped instead of rounded? Generate a cutline from a cut-out PNG first.

Questions

Fridge magnet FAQ

How do I make a fridge magnet from a photo?

Upload the photo, choose the finished long side, check the crop against the rounded corner preview, then download the 300 DPI PNG. The file is what a printer or magnet supplier needs to produce the piece.

What size should a photo fridge magnet be?

A 5 cm magnet is a small keepsake, often used for announcements and party favours. 7 cm is the most useful size for a single photo because faces stay readable from a distance. 9 cm suits group shots, recipes and artwork.

Why do fridge magnets use rounded corners?

Sharp corners on a printed magnet peel and fray first because that is where the surface film lifts. A small corner radius spreads that wear and looks neater, so most photo magnets are produced with rounded or circular corners.

Is the fridge magnet maker free?

Yes. The preview and the PNG download are free, with no account, no email and no watermark. Your photo is processed in your own browser and is not uploaded to us.

Can I use these designs as a small business?

You can use the download however you like for your own artwork. If you plan to sell the printed magnets, keep the final size, corner radius and file resolution consistent, and consider keeping a resolved copy of each design so a reprint matches the original.
