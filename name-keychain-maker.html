What makes a good acrylic name keychain

  • Keep the name short. Up to 16 characters fit, but four to eight characters read best at keyring size.
  • Check the hanging tab. The maker welds a small tab above the letters so the drill hole always lands on solid acrylic, whichever letters you use.
  • Choose the size after you see the preview. The longest side is fixed at 5, 7 or 9 cm and the width follows the name.
  • If you upload a photo, use a bright image with one clear subject so the letters stay readable when they are small.
