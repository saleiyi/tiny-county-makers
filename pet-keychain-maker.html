How to make a pet photo keychain

Upload a pet photo

Eye level, head fully in frame, background reasonably plain. Phone photos are fine as long as the face is sharp — that is the part people look at.

Clean the background

Design rules for pet photos

  • Keep the ears and whiskers inside a slightly thicker border — thin shapes are the first thing to chip.
  • Dark fur on a dark background disappears in a small print. Raise the contrast before you order.
  • One clear subject reads better at 4–6 cm than a group of pets.

Questions

Pet keychain FAQ

How do I make a keychain out of my pet photo?

Upload the photo to the keychain studio, erase the background around your pet, choose the finished size and border, then download the design. Ordering the physical keychain is a separate, optional step that always starts with a proof.

What photo works best for a pet keychain?

A photo taken at your pet's eye level, with the head fully visible and the background reasonably uncluttered, gives the cleanest cut-out. Anything from a phone camera is usually enough, as long as the head is sharp.

Is the pet keychain maker free to use?

Yes. The maker is free, needs no account and adds no watermark. Your photo is edited inside your own browser, and the design download is yours to keep whether or not you order anything.

Can I put two pets in one keychain?

Yes. Upload a photo of the pets together, or erase the background from one photo and place the second subject in the same canvas. Keep both heads at a similar scale so the finished piece still reads clearly.

What happens after I order a physical keychain?

A person reviews your artwork and replies with a proof image. You approve or ask for changes. Only after you approve the proof do we send a PayPal invoice, then the keychain is made by hand and shipped.
