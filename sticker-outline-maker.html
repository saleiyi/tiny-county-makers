Three steps

How to add a white outline to a picture

Upload the artwork

Send in a PNG, JPG or WebP file. A PNG with a transparent background, or a subject photographed on a plain wall, gives the cleanest outline.

Set the border and the size

Pull the border width from 1 mm to 8 mm, choose the colour, and set the longest side to 4, 6, 8 or 10 cm. The outline redraws as you move the sliders.

Download the sticker

Take the 300 DPI PNG with its transparent background, or the SVG when a cutting machine has to follow the outside edge. Both are free and neither carries a watermark.

The whole job takes about a minute, and it is the same outline a designer would build by hand: trace the edge, expand it outwards, fill it, and place the artwork back on top. The one choice that changes everything is the source file. A cut-out PNG honestly gives a better result than a busy photo, because there is a real edge for the tracer to follow.

Reference

Sticker sizes and border widths

The sticker is measured by its longest side, so a tall logo gives a tall sticker and a wide illustration gives a wide one. Start at 6 cm with a 2 mm border; that pairing reads clearly at arm’s length and still cuts cleanly.

Long sideGood forWatch out for
4 cmSticker sheets, phone cases, journal cornersA thick border on a small sticker eats the artwork
6 cmPlanners, laptops, water bottlesKeep the border at 1 to 3 mm so the art still reads
8 cmNotebook covers, scrapbook pages, giftsNeeds a sharp source or the border highlights the blur
10 cmWall decals, packaging labels, party favoursOnly worth it when the artwork has a clean edge

How the outline is drawn

  • If the picture is fully opaque and sits on an even backdrop, that backdrop is detected and removed first, so the border follows the subject instead of the rectangle around it.
  • The remaining silhouette is traced into contours, simplified to drop pixel noise, then smoothed by the amount you choose.
  • The contour is expanded outwards by the border width in millimetres, measured against the real print size rather than the pixels on screen.
  • The expanded shape is filled with your border colour and the original artwork is drawn back over the top, which is why the border sits behind the art and never covers it.

What the download contains

  • A transparent 300 DPI PNG of the sticker: border plus artwork, with nothing else in the frame.
  • An SVG with the border as a closed path called Border, the artwork on its own Artwork layer, and a hairline Edge path showing where the art stops.
  • The artwork at the size you selected on the long side, not stretched and not cropped.
  • No watermark, so the file can go straight into a print order or a sticker machine.

Getting a clean outline

  • Use a PNG with a transparent background when you have one. It is the single biggest quality win.
  • Photographing a cut-out object? Put it on a plain sheet of paper in even light, and the backdrop is lifted for you.
  • Raise the edge smoothing to 6 or 7 when the source is low resolution and the outline looks jagged.
  • Keep the border at or above 1 mm. Below that a home printer cannot hold the line.
  • Want the cutting edge instead of the printed border? Generate a die-cut cutline, or turn the same artwork into an acrylic cake topper.

Questions

Sticker outline FAQ

How do I add a white outline to a picture?

Upload the picture, drag the border width slider to the thickness you want, and pick the colour. The tool traces the outside edge of the artwork, lays a border around it, and gives you a transparent PNG at 300 DPI plus an SVG with the same border as a real path.

Is the sticker outline maker free?

Yes. Uploading artwork, changing the border width and colour, and downloading the PNG or the SVG are free, with no account, no watermark and no limit on how many files you make. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so the image is never uploaded to us.

What kind of picture works best?

Artwork with a transparent background is ideal, and so is a subject photographed against a plain, even wall. The tracer needs a clear edge to follow, so a busy photo where the subject blends into the background gives a loose or rectangular outline. A flat, even backdrop is detected and removed automatically before the trace.

What size should I make the sticker?

The long side runs from 4 cm to 10 cm. Four to six centimetres suits a planner sticker, a phone case or a laptop corner, while eight to ten centimetres suits a water bottle, a notebook cover or a printed sheet of stickers.

Can I use the PNG in Canva, Procreate or a cutting machine?

Yes. The PNG keeps a transparent background at 300 DPI, so it can be dropped straight into Canva, Procreate, Affinity or a print order. If a cutting machine has to follow the outside edge, download the SVG instead, where the border is a closed path and the artwork sits on its own layer.
