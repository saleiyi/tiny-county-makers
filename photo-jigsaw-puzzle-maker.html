Three steps

How to make a jigsaw puzzle from a photo

Upload the photo

Send in a PNG, JPG or WebP picture. A colourful, busy photo makes a harder puzzle than a plain background does.

Pick the pieces and the size

Choose 3 x 3, 4 x 4, 5 x 5 or 6 x 6 pieces and a 15, 20, 25 or 30 cm long side. The preview redraws the tabs and blanks as you change them.

Download and cut

Save the printable 300 DPI PNG with the cut lines on it, or take the SVG cut file to a laser or a cutting machine. Both are free.

The best size for a first puzzle is a 20 cm board with 16 pieces: big enough to read the picture, small enough to finish in one sitting. Go to 5 x 5 or 6 x 6 when the puzzle is for an adult, and stay at 3 x 3 when a small child has to be able to pick up the pieces.

Reference

Jigsaw puzzle sizes and piece counts

The puzzle is measured by the length of the longest side, so the finished board follows the shape of your photo instead of being forced into a square. A tall portrait photo gives a tall puzzle, and a wide landscape photo gives a wide one.

How many pieces should I choose?

  • 3 x 3 (9 pieces) is aimed at small children: the pieces are large and each one carries a big piece of the picture.
  • 4 x 4 (16 pieces) is the safe default and the easiest grid to cut by hand with a craft knife.
  • 5 x 5 (25 pieces) is the sweet spot for a family puzzle or a small gift.
  • 6 x 6 (36 pieces) is the hardest grid we draw. Try it at 25 cm or 30 cm so the pieces stay big enough to handle.

What the download contains

  • A 300 DPI PNG of the finished board with the tabs, blanks and outline already drawn on it, so it can be printed and cut by hand.
  • An SVG cut file with one closed outline and one open path for every interior cut, which is what a laser or a cutting machine reads.
  • The photo fitted to the board without stretching, at the crop you approved in the preview.
  • No watermark, so the file can go straight into a print job or a laser.

Printing and cutting tips

  • Print the PNG at 100% scale rather than "fit to page", otherwise the pieces come out smaller than the size you chose.
  • Glue the print to 1 to 2 mm card before cutting, then cut along the dark lines with a craft knife.
  • Cut the straight outline first, then work inwards one interior line at a time. The tabs and blanks are drawn as one shared line, so both neighbouring pieces match.
  • For a laser, use the SVG and keep the magenta Cutline layer on the cut pass, with the artwork on a separate engraved pass.
