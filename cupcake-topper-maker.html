How many toppers fit on a sheet

  • A US Letter sheet (21.6 x 27.9 cm) holds 12 two inch, 12 two and a half inch or 6 three inch toppers.
  • An A4 sheet (21 x 29.7 cm) holds 15 two inch, 8 two and a half inch or 6 three inch toppers.
  • The tool leaves a 8 mm printer border and a 2.5 mm gutter between toppers, and draws a light dashed line down each gutter so a guillotine can separate the whole run in a few passes.
  • Two inch toppers give the most per sheet, so they are the cheapest option for a large party or a classroom.

The four silhouettes

  • Round is the classic party disc and the shape every 2 inch punch is made for.
  • Scalloped edge adds a frilled border that reads as a hand-cut craft card, with the same outside diameter as the round version.
  • Rounded corner is a soft square, which suits a name, a number or a short message better than a disc does.
  • Square is the easiest shape to trim with a paper cutter and tiles neatly on a sheet.

Card colours and messages

  • White is the safe choice for a colour photo because the card does not tint the picture.
  • Ivory and blush suit weddings, anniversaries and a soft pastel palette.
  • Sage works for a woodland, botanical or baby shower theme, and kraft looks like unbleached card stock.
  • Black makes the message switch to white type, which reads well over a dark or high-contrast photo.
  • The message sits on a band of card at the foot, so it stays readable over any photo. Keep it under about 16 characters for a round 2 inch topper and longer messages will shrink to fit.

Printing and cutting

  • Print the sheet at 100 percent on 200-250 gsm card stock, on the highest quality setting your printer offers.
  • Matte stock soaks up ink and suits the ivory, kraft and blush cards; gloss keeps a colour photo punchier.
  • Trim along the dashed line rather than the solid edge, so no cut mark is left on the printed card.
  • Tape a wooden pick or a paper straw to the back of each topper if you want it to stand up above the frosting.

Cupcake topper FAQ

How do I make a cupcake topper?

Upload a photo, or skip the photo and print a blank card, then pick a 2, 2.5 or 3 inch size and one of the four silhouettes, type a message if you want one, and download the 300 DPI PNG. The preview and the download are drawn from the same recipe, so what you see on screen is what comes out of the printer.

Is the cupcake topper maker free?

Yes. Uploading a photo, changing the size, the shape, the card colour, the font and the message, and downloading a single topper or a whole Letter or A4 sheet as a 300 DPI PNG are all free. There is no account, no watermark and no limit, and the photo never leaves your device.

What size should a cupcake topper be?

Two inches (5.1 cm) is the standard for a single cupcake and the size most party shops sell. Two and a half inches (6.4 cm) reads better in a group photo, and three inches (7.6 cm) suits a large muffin or a cupcake with a tall swirl. A topper looks best when it is a little wider than the frosting, so a 2 inch disc on a 2.5 inch cupcake leaves a clean margin.

How many cupcake toppers fit on one sheet?

A US Letter sheet holds 12 two inch toppers, 12 two and a half inch toppers or 6 three inch toppers. An A4 sheet holds 15 two inch toppers, 8 two and a half inch toppers or 6 three inch toppers. The tool tiles them with a 2.5 mm gutter and light dashed cut lines, so you can trim the whole run apart with a guillotine.

Can I print cupcake toppers at home?

Yes. Download the sheet, print it at 100 percent with no scaling on card stock at the highest quality your printer offers, then cut along the dashed lines. Home inkjet card stock works well for a dozen or so toppers; a copy shop is the better choice for a large party run or for heavier 250 gsm stock, and a colour laser keeps the card from curling.

How do I cut cupcake toppers out?

The four silhouettes are the cut lines: round, scalloped, rounded corner and square. Scissors are fine for a single round disc, but a 2 inch circle punch or a craft knife against a steel rule gives a cleaner edge on a whole sheet. Keep the printed dashed ring as your guide and trim just outside it so no cut line is left on the card.
